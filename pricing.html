FAQ

Straight answers on licensing

For studios, indies and Web3 teams building real products.

Can I use one license on multiple projects?

Yes. Indie and Studio plans cover unlimited commercial projects per seat. Build your RPG and your racing game with the same license. Starter is for prototypes only.

Can I move a seat to another team member?

Licenses are per seat, not per machine. Reassign a seat when someone leaves and the new developer picks up with the same library.

Are console releases covered?

All paid plans include publishing rights on PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo Switch, with no extra royalties or platform fees.

Does this cover Web3 and crypto games?

Yes. Commercial use extends to blockchain games and Web3 apps on the same royalty-free terms.

What if my subscription lapses mid-project?

Everything you downloaded before cancelling stays perpetually licensed for any title you ship. You lose access to new downloads and updates only.
